About

Teaching gave me my first customer service lesson: nobody learns, or trusts, in confusion.

While teaching Biology online, I worked with students in the UK and kept their parents updated each week. I learned to listen for what each person needed, explain things in a way they could understand, and adjust my support rather than assume one approach worked for everyone.

Later, at a front desk in Nigeria, I noticed that people whose concerns were passed to another department were sometimes left without an update. I started telling them when an issue had been escalated and calling back when there was an outcome. The company reported a 5% rise in customer satisfaction, and management recognized the follow-up as part of it. That experience shaped how I work: listen carefully, verify before promising, make clear handoffs, and follow through.

At Stage Orion, I practiced those habits in a hands-on Freshdesk team simulation, handling four ticket scenarios as both a support agent and an escalation officer, and creating two help center articles. I enjoy making support feel clear and human, especially for people navigating work and life across borders. That is the kind of Customer Care work I want to keep growing in.
